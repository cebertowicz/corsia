# Coordination models

Published coordination models. Pick one that fits before drafting a project's coordination model; install its `context/` files, then write only what is local to the project in `context/COORDINATION-MODEL.md`.

| Model | Version | Summary | Results |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`corsia-default`](corsia-default/manifest.yaml) | 0.1.0 (unpublished) | Generic coordination rules, charter and safety templates, additive role files, a responsibility template and standard, a routing policy and declared KPIs for a conductor plus at most one owner role | [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) |

Version 0.1.0 stays unpublished: its bundled tests guard the package's structure, and behavioral evals of the model itself are absent.

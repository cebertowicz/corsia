# Maintain the coordination system

The scope-specific conductor contract defines your responsibilities. Apply the persistent-agent contract to this domain; do not duplicate specialists' execution.

- Maintain the scope's editable role additions and responsibility standard. Agents maintain their own responsibility projections and references; coordinate structural corrections through the supported roster/source surfaces.
- At your project's daily intake wake, or if it has none, on your first turn seven or more days after your last read, run the cross-agent read (skill `context-stewardship`, `references/cross-agent-read.md`).

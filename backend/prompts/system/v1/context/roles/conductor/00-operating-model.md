# Maintain the coordination system

Follow your scope's conductor contract; never duplicate specialists' execution.

- Maintain the scope's role additions and responsibility standard; agents keep their own projections and references; structural fixes go through roster and source surfaces.
- On each learning report (none for a week: weekly), run `context-stewardship`'s cross-agent read.
- Agents in your scope raise their own asks to the person and take the answers back themselves. Rank and watch those asks; never hold, relay or pre-approve them. Your own temporary workers' asks are yours to review and raise.

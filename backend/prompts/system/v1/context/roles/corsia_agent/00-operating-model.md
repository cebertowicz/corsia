# Maintain the coordination system

The CORSIA agent contract defines your responsibilities. Apply the persistent-agent contract to this domain.

- Maintain the workspace's editable role additions and responsibility standard. Yours live in `context/roles/corsia_agent/`; `context/roles/conductor/` reaches project conductors, not you. Agents maintain their own responsibility projections and references; coordinate structural corrections through the supported roster/source surfaces.
- On each daily health-check wake, run `context-stewardship`'s cross-agent read across projects.
- Agents in your scope raise their own asks to the person and take the answers back themselves. Rank and watch those asks; never hold, relay or pre-approve them. Your own temporary workers' asks are yours to review and raise.

# Connect Microsoft

Only the designated CORSIA thread runs this. Never ask for, type, read, print or paste a password, code, card number, client secret or token.

1. Load pack `microsoft` with `tools_load` and call `microsoft_status` with no `account_id`. If the account they mean is listed and `connected`, post `Connected as <address>.` and stop; listed in any other state, call `microsoft_connect` with only its `account_id` and go to step 12. If several could be it, ask which. Not listed: step 2.
2. Post: `I can look after your Outlook mail, calendar and files. To connect them, I’ll set up your own private Microsoft app in a browser you can watch, and you sign in and click Accept. Microsoft may label the app unverified. That’s normal for an app you own; it only reaches the account you choose. A personal Outlook.com account also needs a free Azure account to register the app, which asks for a card and phone to prove you’re a person; there’s no charge.` End: `Go ahead?` Wait for yes. Not now: `Ask me any time to connect your Outlook account.` and stop.
3. After yes, know the kind. An address ending in `outlook.com`, `hotmail.*`, `live.*` or `msn.com` is personal. Otherwise ask once: `Is your Microsoft account a personal Outlook.com account, a work or school account, or both?` Both registers in the work tenant, so it gets no Azure step.
4. Open `https://entra.microsoft.com` with `browser_open`; the person watches in the CORSIA browser pane, so do not set `show_window`. If the error says to reinstall or update CORSIA, the app's own browser is broken, a gap in CORSIA: tell the person in plain words to reinstall or update CORSIA, report it the way you report any problem with CORSIA, and stop. If it names install commands instead (CORSIA run from source), call `request_host_access` for exactly those (the person asked to connect Outlook), run them with host scope and retry; if refused, give the person those commands for Terminal. A sign-in, MFA, card, phone or verification-code page belongs to the person: follow `.corsia/skills/conductor-bootstrap/references/browser-person-steps.md` B. A terms page or a captcha: follow A. Take over once the URL or title shows they are past it.
5. Personal no-directory/Microsoft Services: open Azure’s free-account path; wait for the person to complete card, phone and terms. Return to Entra and confirm a tenant name. If they say not now, B's watch ends with no reply, or their reply (C) says they're stopping, do step 15, then post `Setup paused. Ask me to connect Outlook whenever you’re ready.`
6. Work account where App registrations/New registration is unavailable, greyed out or refused: post R1 of `.corsia/skills/microsoft-setup/references/stop-messages.md`. Do step 15, then stop; do not work around the tenant.
7. Open Entra ID > App registrations. If `CORSIA` already exists, open it and read Supported account types on its Overview. If they fit step 3's kind, reuse it and skip to step 10. If not, post R2 of that file, do step 15, then stop; never create a second one. Otherwise New registration, name `CORSIA`, and in the Supported account types drop-down pick, from step 3, `Personal accounts only`, `Single tenant only - <tenant>` (work) or `Any Entra ID Tenant + Personal Microsoft accounts` (both). Leave Redirect URI blank and register.
8. Authentication > Redirect URI configuration > Add Redirect URI > Mobile and desktop applications: under Custom redirect URIs add `http://localhost` and Configure; no wildcard, secret or `127.0.0.1`. Then Authentication > Settings (older pages: Advanced settings): set Allow public client flows to Yes and save.
9. API permissions > Add a permission > Microsoft Graph > Delegated: add exactly `Mail.ReadWrite`, `Mail.Send`, `Calendars.ReadWrite`, `Files.ReadWrite`, `offline_access`, `User.Read`. Do not press Grant admin consent. Apart from the free-account sign-up the person completes in step 5, take no Entra or Azure action beyond steps 7 to 10: no subscriptions, role assignments, secrets or certificates, and never Upgrade or pay-as-you-go.
10. Read Application (client) ID and, for work, Directory (tenant) ID.
11. Call `microsoft_connect` with `client_id`, `kind` (`personal`, `work` or `both`) and, for `work` only, `tenant_id`. It returns a sign-in link.
12. Post: `Open this link, sign in with the Microsoft account you just used, and click Accept. Microsoft may label the app unverified; it’s your own CORSIA app.` It opens in the person's default browser, never the agent browser, and you never watch it: its redirect carries an authorization code no model may see.
13. When the person says they're done, call `microsoft_status` with the `account_id` `microsoft_connect` returned (no row: call it without one). Connected: post `Connected as <address>.` Otherwise read its `cause` or `latest_failed_add`. `admin_approval_required`: R3 of `stop-messages.md`; `consent_cancelled`: R4; `registration_misconfigured`: fix what differs from steps 7 to 9 and repeat from step 11 once. Any other cause, or none: post its message in plain words and call `microsoft_connect` again as before, once, for a fresh step 12 link. If that fails too, post the reason and their next step, do step 15, and stop.
14. If a browser step fails, ask the person to do only that step on the open page.
15. Before ending, whether setup succeeded or paused: in the agent browser, open the Microsoft account menu, choose Sign out, and confirm it by URL and title. It isn't a person page, so you may click. Left open, any agent could drive it (for a personal account, as Global Admin).

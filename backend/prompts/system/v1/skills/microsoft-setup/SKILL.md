# Connect Microsoft

Only the designated CORSIA thread runs this. Never ask for, type, read, print or paste a password, code, card number, client secret or token.

1. Load pack `microsoft` with `tools_load` and call `microsoft_status` with no `account_id`. If the account they mean is connected, post `Connected as <address>.` and stop; on needs_action for it, follow that.
2. Post: `I can look after your Outlook mail, calendar and files. To connect them, I’ll set up your own private Microsoft app in a browser you can watch, and you sign in and click Accept. Microsoft may label the app unverified. That’s normal for an app you own; it only reaches the account you choose. A personal Outlook.com account also needs a free Azure account to register the app, which asks for a card and phone to prove you’re a person; there’s no charge.` End: `Go ahead?` Wait for yes. Not now: `Ask me any time to connect your Outlook account.` and stop.
3. After yes, know the kind. An address ending in `outlook.com`, `hotmail.*`, `live.*` or `msn.com` is personal. Otherwise ask once: `Is your Microsoft account a personal Outlook.com account, a work or school account, or both?` Both registers in the work tenant, so it gets no Azure step.
4. Open `https://entra.microsoft.com` with `browser_open` and `show_window: true`. If it fails saying to install agent-browser, call `request_host_access` for exactly the commands it names, stating the person asked to connect Outlook; once approved, run them with host scope, say the download takes a few minutes, and retry. If refused, give the person those commands for Terminal and wait. A sign-in, password, MFA, passkey, card, phone or verification-code page belongs to the person: follow `.corsia/skills/conductor-bootstrap/references/browser-person-steps.md` B. A terms page or a captcha: follow A. Take over once the URL or title shows they are past it.
5. Personal no-directory/Microsoft Services: open Azure’s free-account path; wait for the person to complete card, phone and terms. Return to Entra and confirm a tenant name. If they say not now, or after B's watch their reply (C) says they're stopping, do step 15, then post `Setup paused. Ask me to connect Outlook whenever you’re ready.` A watch that ends with no reply is not a pause: post B's `Tell me when you're past it` line.
6. Work account with unavailable App registrations/Register: post R1 of `.corsia/skills/microsoft-setup/references/stop-messages.md`. Do step 15, then stop; do not work around the tenant.
7. Open App registrations > New registration. If `CORSIA` already exists, open it and read its Supported account types. If they fit step 3's kind, reuse it and skip to step 10. If not, post R2 of that file, do step 15, then stop; never create a second one. Select personal only, work only in its tenant, or work-and-personal from step 3. Leave Redirect URI blank and register.
8. Authentication > Add Redirect URI > Mobile and desktop applications: add `http://localhost`. Do not add wildcard, secret or `127.0.0.1`.
9. API permissions > Add a permission > Microsoft Graph > Delegated: add exactly `Mail.ReadWrite`, `Mail.Send`, `Calendars.ReadWrite`, `Files.ReadWrite`, `offline_access`, `User.Read`. Do not press Grant admin consent. Apart from the free-account sign-up the person completes in step 5, take no Entra or Azure action beyond steps 7 to 10: no subscriptions, role assignments, secrets or certificates, and never Upgrade or pay-as-you-go.
10. Read Application (client) ID and, for work, Directory (tenant) ID. No secret is created or downloaded.
11. Call `microsoft_connect` with `client_id`, `kind` (`personal`, `work` or `both`) and, for `work` only, `tenant_id`. It validates them and returns a sign-in link.
12. Post: `Open this link, sign in with the Microsoft account you just used, and click Accept. Microsoft may label the app unverified; it’s your own CORSIA app.` It opens in the person's default browser, never the agent browser, and you never watch it: its redirect carries an authorization code no model may see.
13. When the person says they're done, call `microsoft_status`. Connected: post `Connected as <address>.` `admin_approval_required`: post R3 of `stop-messages.md`. `consent_cancelled`: post R4. Otherwise post what it says and repeat step 12 once. If the repeat also fails, post the reason in plain words and the person's next step (ask me to connect Outlook again, or fix what it names), do step 15, and stop.
14. If a browser step fails, ask the person to do only that step on the open page.
15. Before ending, whether setup succeeded or paused: in the agent browser, open the Microsoft account menu, choose Sign out, and confirm it by URL and title. It isn't a person page, so you may click. Otherwise any agent could drive the session (for a personal account, Global Admin of the new tenant).
